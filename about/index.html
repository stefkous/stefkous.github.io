My name is Stephania Kousoula. I am a Software Engineer specialising in Python, simulation modelling and data engineering.

I currently work as a Level 2 Modelling Engineer at Kallikor | Improbable, developing Python simulation components and reusable data-processing infrastructure. Earlier, at Bryden Wood, I worked on Python ETL pipelines, geospatial analysis, backend services and simulation prototypes.

I hold a Master in City and Technology from the Institute for Advanced Architecture of Catalonia (IAAC), accredited through Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), and a BArch & MSc in Architectural Engineering from the Polytechnic School at Democritus University of Thrace (DUTH).

My engineering work focuses on reusable software, reliable data pipelines, simulation testing and practical customer outcomes. My interdisciplinary background in architectural engineering and urban technology informs how I approach complex systems.
